Our work

Paying for a child's school year

Our main work is simple: we pay school fees for children who would otherwise have to stop going to school.

School scholarships

Who we help

Many children in Sindhupalchowk have lost one or both parents. Others live with families who cannot afford the yearly school fees.

We pay those fees so the children can stay in school. We have done this every year since 2015.

How it works

  1. 1

    We listen

    Village leaders and school headmasters tell us which children need help most.

  2. 2

    We pay the school fees

    The foundation pays the child's yearly school fees.

  3. 3

    The child stays in school

    The child can keep going to school and keep learning.

Disaster help

Help when disasters hit

Nepal has earthquakes, floods and landslides. When disaster hits a community we know, we help where we can.
